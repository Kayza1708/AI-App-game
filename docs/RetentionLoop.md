# Retention Loop

Daily, weekly, and monthly missions are generated from a stable hash of local player identity, period ID, and period type. Reloading keeps the same missions; a new UTC period rotates them. Pools cover Economy, Hardware, Training, Models, Research, Market, Energy, Items, Prestige, and Exploration, while feature gates prevent impossible assignments.

Targets snapshot a baseline when generated. Credits, Compute, Users, and Research scale from bounded current production rather than obsolete fixed amounts. The Daily set contains three primary goals and one optional bonus where enough categories exist. Weekly sets contain up to four broader goals and Monthly sets up to two account goals.

Claiming every primary Daily increments a forgiving completion streak once per period. Consecutive completions continue it; a missed period restarts at one without penalties. Day-three multiples grant small Gems and day-seven multiples grant a gameplay Weekly Cache.

The intended return loop is: reconcile offline production → inspect one aggregated Welcome Back reward → review completed missions → claim an Item/Gem/cache → compare equipment → restart Training → choose the next short, medium, and long horizon.
