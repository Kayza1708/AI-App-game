# Model Builds

Every Model now declares an archetype, strengths, weaknesses, tags, and extensible Item slots. Equipped Item and Set effects feed the same canonical economy used by Hardware, Market, Training, Research, Energy, and telemetry.

The Build Summary reports real equipment and modifiers, then describes the strongest observed axis: Research, Consumer, Enterprise, Agent, or Efficiency. It is descriptive and grants no hidden score or bonus. A Consumer TinyChat can favor adoption and inference, Research builds scientific datasets, Enterprise builds revenue and trust, Agent builds autonomy, and Compute builds training or hardware efficiency.

Model skills, identity, Patents, Tech, Items, Artifacts, events, and future systems are combined through the modifier boundary rather than UI-specific calculations. Additional tag synergies can be authored without changing inventory persistence.
